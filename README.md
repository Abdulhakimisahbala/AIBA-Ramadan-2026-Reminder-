<h2>The Last 10 Days of Ramadan</h2>

<p>
The last ten days of Ramadan are the most blessed days of the holy month.
Muslims are encouraged to increase their worship, prayers, recitation of the Qur'an,
charity, and remembrance of Allah.
</p>

<p>
Among these nights is <strong>Laylatul Qadr (The Night of Power)</strong>,
which is better than a thousand months. Therefore, every Muslim should strive
to seek this night by praying, making dua, and asking Allah for forgiveness.
</p>

<p>Important acts during the last 10 days:</p>

<ul style="text-align:left">
<li>Increase Tahajjud (night prayers)</li>
<li>Recite the Qur'an frequently</li>
<li>Make sincere dua and ask Allah for forgiveness</li>
<li>Give charity to the needy</li>
<li>Remember Allah (Dhikr)</li>
</ul>

<p>
May Allah accept our fasting, prayers, and good deeds in Ramadan 2026. Ameen.
</p>
